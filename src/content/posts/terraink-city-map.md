I used [Terraink](https://terraink.app/) to generate a poster-style map of Calliano, the town I now live in, for my [about page](https://me.federicod.dev/about). It's a free tool that pulls OpenStreetMap data for a location you pick, then renders it as a clean, minimal map you can restyle (colors, labels, roads vs. no roads) and export as a poster or wallpaper.

You don't have to sign up or hunt down a watermark, just pick a spot on the map and tweak it until it looks right.

![Map poster of Calliano](/images/posts/calliano-map.webp)

The designs are nice enough that I could see this hanging on a wall, maybe printed on metal like those Displate pieces.
