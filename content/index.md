This is my digital garden 🌼 a growing collection of notes, ideas, and half-finished thoughts, kept public and linked together instead of locked in a private vault.

Unlike a blog, entries here aren't polished posts frozen in time. They're living notes: added quickly, revisited often, and connected to each other as understanding grows. The goal is to think in the open, build a personal knowledge base that compounds over time, and make it easy to stumble across a note in a different context than the one it was written in.

Browse the graph, follow the links, and expect things to change or break.
