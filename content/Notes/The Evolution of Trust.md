> [!quote]
> "The Nice Guy Strategy wins, over time, in a world with noise, but only if forgiveness has a limit."

Interactive game-theory essay on iterated [[Prisoner's Dilemma]], but playable instead of just diagrammed, you run the tournament yourself and watch "Copycat" strategies win and then collapse under noise.

What stuck with me: forgiveness needs a cap. A strategy that forgives infinitely gets exploited into extinction; the winning variants forgive occasionally, not always. Feels like a decent model for how much slack to give a flaky supplier before switching, not just an abstract game result.
